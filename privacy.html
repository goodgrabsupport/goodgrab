Good Grab: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Good Grab is a game by Flöki Games, operated by Rodrigo Motta Mendes, Estonia ("we"). Contact: goodgrabsupport@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, your email or your contacts.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone only. We have no server and we never receive it.
  • The game shows video ads from Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one. Google's ad software collects some data about your device to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your card or your Apple account.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your coins, your prizes, your unlocked machines, your settings and a list of finished purchases (so that nothing is delivered twice). This file stays on your device (and in your own device backup, if you use one). Deleting the game deletes it from the phone.

Ads (Google AdMob)

When you tap "Watch a video", the game shows a rewarded ad from Google AdMob. To load, show and measure ads, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • a device identifier (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking, see below);
  • your rough location, worked out from your IP address;
  • which ads you saw and how you interacted with them;
  • performance data and crash logs of the ad software.

Google uses this for advertising, for measuring ads and for keeping its service working. Google is responsible for this data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not receive this data. We only see totals in our AdMob account, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • Tracking. At the first start iOS may ask whether the game may track you. If you say no, ads are not based on your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent. Where the law asks for it, Google's consent message lets you choose how your data is used for ads.
  • The game plays the same whatever you choose. You never get or lose coins for your choice.
  • You never have to watch an ad. Ads are an optional way to get free coins.

Purchases

Coins and machines are bought through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment under Apple's Privacy Policy. The game only gets a receipt that says which item was bought, and keeps it on your phone.

Children

Good Grab is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets), and it is not in the App Store's Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing we can look up, correct or delete. For data held by Google or Apple, use their privacy pages linked above. Questions: goodgrabsupport@gmail.com.

If you live in the European Union you can also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).
