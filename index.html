Good Grab: Help

Good Grab is a claw machine game by Flöki Games.

Write to us

Questions, ideas or a problem with the game: goodgrabsupport@gmail.com. We read every message.

A purchase did not arrive

  • Open the game's settings (the gear) and tap Restore purchases. A machine you bought comes back.
  • Coins arrive by themselves the next time the game starts, if the purchase went through.
  • Still missing? Write to goodgrabsupport@gmail.com and tell us what you bought and when. Refunds are handled by Apple: Request a refund.

Privacy

The game has no accounts and keeps your progress on your phone only. Read the Privacy Policy.
